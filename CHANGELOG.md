# Duskfall v0.1.0

## WoW Forever

### New
- Duskfall sets Gamma, Brightness, Contrast and Bloom for where you are: subzone, zone, instance or instance type, indoors or out, time of day, combat, mounted, swimming, underwater, resting and ghost.
- Places are found as you play. A place is off until you give it a Look, and a Look can be for every character or only the ones you pick.
- Styles: named sets of Looks, each with its own transition. Instant, Fade, or Adapt, which works like your eyes: a short dip in the dark, a short glare in the light.
- A settings window with Places, a Look editor with live preview, Styles, Settings and About.
- The minimap's day and night moon opens Duskfall. A minimap button shows up when the moon is hidden. Duskfall is also in the addon compartment and on broker bars.
- An optional floating display shows the Look in effect when it changes.
- Your own settings come back when you log out or switch Duskfall off.
